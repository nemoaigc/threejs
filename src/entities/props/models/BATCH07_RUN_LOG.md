### Corrections made from direct comparison

- Potter: the first operator zone let the stool and water bucket crowd the treadle. Both were moved outward, the bucket staves were closed, and a wet natural sponge was added to the shaping-tool layer.
- Dyeing station: the initial low camera hid both dye baths and made the vats read as dry barrels. Main and side cameras were raised; stave spacing was closed while preserving separate liquid and wet-cloth material response.
- Cooper: the first horizontal barrel read as an open rib cage. Staves were widened, a dark inner sleeve and end shade were added, and the rope clamp gained a visible tail and knot. Fresh oak was separated from the aged bench palette.
- Bake oven: the first mouth was too bright and shallow, while the first roof revision produced floating horizontal bars. The cavity received a dark back and side liners, arch bricks gained soot-darkened upper courses, and the roof was rebuilt as a layered 4×7 tile field with a compact chimney cap. Flour dust now breaks the table surface.
